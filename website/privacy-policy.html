Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal information and upholding your rights under Australian privacy law. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Paul Harvie Orthopaedics handles your personal information in connection with our specialist medical services. We collect, use and disclose personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. By engaging our services, you consent to the practices described here.

Personal information we collect

We collect only what is reasonably necessary to identify you, manage your care, and run our practice effectively.

Identity & contact

  • Name, date of birth, address
  • Phone numbers and email address
  • Medicare and private health fund details
  • Emergency contact information

Health information

  • Medical history, medications, allergies
  • Referrals, specialist reports, test results
  • Surgical bookings and hospital discharge summaries

Administrative & billing

  • Appointment history and preferences
  • Invoices, payment records, Health Insurance Commission claims
  • Communications with you (pre/post-operative calls, emails)

Sensitive information

We may collect other sensitive information necessary for your care with your consent or as permitted by law.

How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide safe, high-quality specialist medical care tailored to you
  • Identify your medical record and coordinate your care throughout your treatment
  • Manage practice operations, including pre-operative and post-operative communications
  • Arrange hospital admissions and surgical bookings in collaboration with hospital staff
  • Handle billing and Medicare/private health fund claims and compliance
  • Respond to your queries and communicate results and recommendations

When we disclose your information

We disclose personal information to third parties involved in your care or as required by law. Typical disclosures include:

Care providers

  • Referring GPs and other specialists
  • Hospitals, surgical booking teams, pathology and imaging providers
  • Allied health professionals directly involved in your care

Legal & administrative

  • Medicare, health insurers and payment processors for claims and audits
  • Regulators or courts where required or permitted by law
  • IT support providers under strict confidentiality to maintain systems

We will obtain your further consent if we need to use or disclose your information for purposes other than those described above, unless permitted or required by law.

Due to privacy laws, results cannot be provided to a third party unless we have your written authorisation or where special circumstances under law apply.

Your rights and choices

  • You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, subject to permitted exemptions. We will provide reasons if access is refused.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate, out-of-date or incomplete information.
  • You may decline to provide information, however this may affect the quality or safety of the care we can provide.
  • You may tell us your communication preferences (e.g., phone, SMS, email). Choosing email may involve offshore processing by service providers.
  • You may withdraw consent for certain uses where not required by law or clinical necessity.

Consent

By attending appointments and providing your information, you consent to our collection, use and disclosure of your personal information as outlined in this policy, subject to any limits you notify us of. We will seek additional consent if a new purpose arises.

You acknowledge

  • The reasons your information must be collected and used for your care
  • That our practice has a privacy policy and can provide a copy on request
  • That, if information is withheld, your care may be impacted

We commit

  • To only collect what is needed and keep it accurate and secure
  • To get your further consent for uses not covered here, unless legally required
  • To provide reasons if we must decline an access request

Security, storage and retention

  • We use technical and organisational safeguards such as access controls, encryption in transit, secure backups and staff confidentiality obligations.
  • Records are stored in secure clinical systems hosted in Australia where practical, or with reputable providers subject to appropriate protections.
  • We retain records as required by healthcare record-keeping laws and professional standards, after which they are securely destroyed or de-identified.

Overseas transfers

If you choose email or certain digital services for communication, your information may be processed or stored outside Australia by contracted providers. We take reasonable steps to ensure overseas recipients do not breach the Australian Privacy Principles in relation to your information.

Questions or complaints

If you have questions about this policy or wish to make a privacy request or complaint, please contact our Privacy Officer. We will acknowledge and respond within a reasonable timeframe.
