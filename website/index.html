About A/Prof Paul Harvie

A/Prof Paul Harvie is a Hobart-based orthopaedic surgeon with a clinical focus on hip and knee conditions. His practice emphasizes clear communication, evidence-based care, and collaboration with patients to achieve functional outcomes.

Services Overview

Refer or Book
Hip Replacement

Primary hip arthroplasty tailored to anatomy and function, aiming for pain relief and mobility restoration.

Knee Replacement

Total or partial knee arthroplasty for end-stage arthritis, focusing on stability and rehabilitation pathways.

Revision Surgery

Evaluation and correction of failed or painful joint replacements, including complex reconstructions.

Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive assessment and treatment of intra-articular pathology where clinically indicated.

Structured information for preparation, recovery timelines, wound care, and safe return to activity.

Second Opinions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?

Yes. A GP or specialist referral is required for Medicare claims and to ensure relevant clinical information is provided.

Where are surgeries performed?

Surgeries are performed at appropriate accredited facilities. Details will be provided with your bookings and pre-admission paperwork.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Referral, imaging reports, medication list, and any prior operative notes. Wear comfortable clothing for examination.

How do I access patient brochures?

Brochures are available above in the Patient Information section. If a specific resource is missing, email the rooms for assistance.
