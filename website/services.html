What We Offer

Four core service areas designed around your needs. Each includes a clear description, expected benefits, and what you can expect during your care.

Hip & Knee Surgery

Joint replacement · Revision surgery · Arthroscopy

Specialist surgical care for osteoarthritis, trauma, and complex joint conditions. Procedures are planned using modern imaging and implant systems to optimise outcomes and longevity.

Benefits
  • Pain reduction and improved mobility
  • Evidence-based implant selection
  • Enhanced recovery protocols
What to Expect
  • Pre-operative assessment and planning
  • Clear guidance on anaesthesia and pain control
  • Structured physiotherapy after surgery

Tailored surgical plans minimise risk and support faster recovery.

Consultations & Assessments

Diagnosis · Imaging review · Treatment planning

In-depth evaluation of symptoms, functional goals, and lifestyle. We utilise clinical examination and imaging to recommend surgical and non-surgical options.

Benefits
  • Clear diagnosis and personalised plan
  • Discuss risks, alternatives, and expectations
  • Streamlined care pathway
What to Expect
  • History, examination, and imaging review
  • Discussion of suitable options
  • Written summary and next steps

Your goals shape the treatment plan from day one.

Surgical Procedures Overview

Techniques · Anaesthesia · Safety

We outline the surgical journey including anaesthesia options, incision placement, implant fixation, and intra-operative checks to ensure precision and safety.

Benefits
  • Transparent explanation of steps
  • Reduced anxiety through preparation
  • Improved outcomes with validated protocols
What to Expect
  • Pre-op instructions and fasting guidance
  • Anaesthetic review and consent
  • Post-op monitoring and early mobilisation

Safety-first approach with consultant oversight throughout.

Post‑operative Care & Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy · Pain control · Follow-up

Structured recovery plans tailored to your procedure and goals. We coordinate physiotherapy, wound care, and review milestones to ensure safe, steady progress.

Benefits
  • Faster return to function
  • Optimised pain management plans
  • Clinician-monitored progress
What to Expect
  • Daily exercises and progress checks
  • Wound care and red‑flag awareness
  • Follow‑up appointments at set intervals

Personalised rehabilitation plans to support a confident recovery.

Patient Information

Practical details to help you prepare and feel informed at each step of your care.

Before Your Appointment
  • Bring previous imaging and medication list
  • Wear comfortable clothing for examination
  • Arrive 10 minutes early for forms
After Your Surgery
  • Follow wound care and exercise guidelines
  • Know red flags: fever, calf pain, excessive swelling
  • Keep follow‑up appointments for review
Timelines & Recovery
  • Day 0–3: pain relief and gentle mobility
  • Week 2–6: progressive exercises and milestones
  • Month 3+: return to low‑impact activities
