About A/Prof Paul Harvie

A/Prof Paul Harvie is an orthopaedic surgeon specialising in hip and knee conditions, joint replacement, revision surgery, and arthroscopy. He is committed to providing personalised, evidence-based care with clear communication and thoughtful recovery planning.

Fellowship trained Hip & Knee focus Patient-centered care

Biography

A/Prof Paul Harvie is an experienced orthopaedic surgeon with a dedicated focus on hip and knee surgery. His clinical interests include total joint replacement, complex revision surgery, arthroscopy, and minimally invasive techniques. He is passionate about restoring mobility and independence for his patients through tailored treatment plans and collaborative decision-making.

He completed advanced training and fellowships in joint reconstruction and revision surgery, and maintains an active role in teaching, research, and quality improvement. Patients value his calm, thoughtful approach and clear communication throughout the surgical journey.

Clinical Focus

  • Hip & knee joint replacement (primary and revision)
  • Arthroscopy and soft-tissue reconstruction
  • Enhanced recovery and pain optimisation
