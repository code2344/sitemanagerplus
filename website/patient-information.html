Frequently Asked Questions

When should I stop eating and drinking before surgery?Show
Follow the fasting guidelines in the Surgery Preparation Checklist from Paul Harvie Orthopaedics. Typically, nothing to eat after midnight and clear liquids up to 2 hours before arrival, unless your surgeon advises otherwise.
How do I manage pain after surgery?Show
Use the multimodal pain plan in the Post‑Operative Care guide. Take medications as prescribed, use ice/heat as directed, and contact Paul Harvie Orthopaedics if your pain is worsening or uncontrolled.
When can I start rehab exercises?Show
Most protocols begin with gentle range‑of‑motion and isometrics within a few days, progressing per your surgeon’s timeline. Refer to the Rehabilitation Exercises brochure for phase‑specific guidance approved by Paul Harvie Orthopaedics.
What symptoms should I report immediately?Show
Fever over 101°F, increasing redness or drainage at the incision, severe calf pain/swelling, chest pain, or shortness of breath. Call our office right away using the phone number below.
